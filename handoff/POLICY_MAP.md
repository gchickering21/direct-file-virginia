# Policy map: Virginia Form 760 lines → law → facts → screens → checks

Written for a Virginia policy analyst or engineer. One row per rule the prototype
implements. Tax year 2024. Fact paths are in
`form-builder-examples/direct-file/src/main/resources/direct-file/facts/virginia.xml`
(the locality ones in `facts/virginiaLocalities.xml` beside it); the Virginia screens are in
`flow/your-taxes-virginia.xml`. "Federal facts read" names what the rule consumes from the
federal return, and how the federal return exports it to a state: **MeF** (in the Form 1040
XML) or **state systems** (the state API's enriched JSON). `scripts/check-virginia-docs.py`
fails if a path named here is not a fact in the XML, and warns if a Form 760 line a fact
names has no row here; `scripts/check-virginia-exports.py` fails if a rule reads a federal
fact that is not exported.

The hand-check column gives the four demo households in order: Riley (Demo 1), Morgan
(Demo 2), Jordan (Demo 3), Taylor (Demo 4). The households are at the end of this page.

## The four hand checks

The households are fictional. Lines 20 to 22 come from the records stand-in on the page; the
scenario files carry $0 there, which is why the ScalaTest pins the demos before those payments.

**Riley Rappahannock (Demo 1)**, the IRS's saved scenario "Single 33k No Credits" with the
residence answer changed to Virginia: single, born 1997-10-03, wages $33,333, $300 withheld,
federal AGI $34,333; Richmond City; $400 of untaxed purchases; Virginia's records show $200
of estimated payments. Line 7 $0 → Line 9 $34,333 → Line 11 $8,500 → Line 12 $930 → Line 15
$24,903 → Line 16: $720 + 5.75% × ($24,903 − $17,000) = $1,174.42, rounded $1,174 → less
$300 withheld and $200 estimated, Line 27 $674 → Line 33 $400 × 6% = $24 → Line 35
**owe $698**.

**Morgan Shenandoah (Demo 2)**, the same scenario with the birth date 1955-05-05 and wages
$56,000: federal AGI $57,000; 65 or older; AFAGI $57,000, $7,000 over the $50,000
threshold, so the age deduction is $12,000 − $7,000 = $5,000; Line 9 $52,000; Line 11
$8,500; Line 12 $930 + $800 = $1,730; Line 13 $3,000 into a Commonwealth Savers account;
Line 15 $38,770; Line 16: $720 + 5.75% × $21,770 = $1,971.78, rounded $1,972 → less $300
withheld and the $150 overpayment Virginia carried over, Line 35 **owe $1,522**. A credit
is never in play: no EIC, and family VAGI is far over the guideline.

**Jordan Elizabeth (Demo 3)**: head of household, born 1975-07-19, blind, one foster child
claimed; W-2 wages with $200 withheld and two $1,000 1099-Gs, federal AGI $21,500; a $4,213
federal earned income credit; Norfolk City from ZIP 23504. Line 7 $2,000 (unemployment, code 37) →
Line 9 $19,500 → Line 11 $8,500 → Line 12 $930 × 2 = $1,860 with the $800 blind exemption
given up → Line 15 $9,140 → Line 16: $120 + 5% × $4,140 = $327. Credit: family VAGI
$19,500 is at or under the $20,440 guideline for two, so $600; 20% of $4,213 = $843,
usable only to $327; 15% = $632, refundable. Keeping the blind exemption would make the
tax $287, and $327 − $632 = −$305 beats it, so the return claims the $632. Line 26 $832 →
Line 28 $505 → Line 36 **refund $505**.

**Taylor York (Demo 4)**: single, born 1997-10-03, on extended active duty; wages $27,000,
$650 withheld, federal AGI $28,000; $24,000 of basic pay; ZIP 23185, which crosses three
localities, so the screen asks. Line 7: $15,000 − ($24,000 − $15,000) = $6,000 (code 38) →
Line 9 $22,000 → Line 15 $12,570 → Line 16: $120 + 5% × $7,570 = $498.50, rounded $499 →
less $650 withheld, Line 28 $151 → Line 36 **refund $151**.

The engine gives exactly these on the Virginia summary page, and the 50 households of
`scripts/virginia-cases.py` (these four among them) agree with the independent model in
`scripts/lib/virginia-model.cjs` on every line.

## Not modelled, and why

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| The Virginia Lottery subtraction, Schedule ADJ code 24 (§ 58.1-322.02(7)) | Removed. The Code subtracts only an amount "included therein", in federal AGI, from a prize under $600. Direct File has no gambling or other-income fact, so no prize is ever on Line 1, and a filer with one is outside Direct File's scope; a question could only subtract what was never added. The earlier version asked it; the comment at the subtractions block in `virginia.xml` records the removal |
| Line 19b, the spouse's withholding on a joint return | Which filer a W-2 belongs to is not among the exported facts. The total on Line 19 is right; the split is not derivable. A state would match each W-2's employee TIN (exported) against the filers' TINs |
| Line 6, the state income tax refund (§ 58.1-322.02(5)) | Direct File has no fact for a taxable state refund (Form 1040 Schedule 1 line 1); nothing to read |
| Interest on U.S. obligations (§ 58.1-322.02(1)) | Not yet. Direct File exports each 1099-INT's box 3 (`/interestReports/*/interestOnGovernmentBonds`) to states, so this is the next subtraction to take, the way the unemployment one was |
| Line 2, additions (Schedule ADJ) | Interest on other states' bonds and the federal deductions Virginia adds back; the demo households have none, and the conformity additions need Virginia Tax's reading of the year's conformity act |
| Line 17, the spouse tax adjustment | Needs each spouse's share of Virginia AGI; the federal per-filer wage facts are not exported |
| Itemized deductions (Line 10), the other Schedule ADJ deductions, Lines 24 and 25, Lines 29 to 32, part-year and nonresident returns, married filing separately on a combined return, the carryforward of excess 529 contributions, a dependent's own income in family VAGI (taken as $0) | Out of scope for the prototype; the gap list in `form-builder-examples/direct-file/docs/virginia/README.md` orders them by size |
